L'aurora is a highly rated, intimate holiday apartment located in San Vito dei Normanni, a charming town in the Puglia region of southern Italy. Situated less than a kilometer from the historic center and the iconic Castello Dentice di Frasso, it provides an excellent home base for exploring both the Apulian countryside and the nearby Adriatic coast.
Property Overview & Amenities
The property is managed by a private host and is tailored for couples or solo travelers looking for a quiet, self-sufficient stay.
• Space: A cozy 23 m² layout that efficiently features one bedroom, one bathroom, and a private entrance.
• Outdoor Area: Includes both a private balcony and access to terraces (covered and open) offering city or garden views.
• Kitchen: Fully equipped with a refrigerator, stovetop, coffee machine, and a minibar, making it easy to prepare your own meals.
• Comforts: Outfitted with private air conditioning, soundproofing, high-speed Wi-Fi (suitable for video calls), and a flat-screen TV.
• Logistics: Offers self-check-in conveniences and street parking under video surveillance. Note that pets, smoking, and hosting parties are prohibited.
Location & Nearby Attractions
Staying here places you in a authentic, less-crowded part of Puglia while keeping major landmarks within a short driving distance:
• Torre Guaceto Reserve: The pristine beaches and protected marine areas of the Torre Guaceto Nature Reserve are about 22 km away.
• Costa Merlata: Gorgeous rocky coves and beaches are roughly 21 km from the property.
• Ostuni & Brindisi: The famous "White City" of Ostuni and the historical landmarks of Brindisi (including the Colonna di Sant'Oronzo) are easily accessible within a 15-to-20 minute drive.
• Transit: The closest major transit hub is the Brindisi - Salento Airport, located roughly 27 km away.
